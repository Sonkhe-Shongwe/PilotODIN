## Development and verification flow

1. Read the handoff for the next authorised task and the scorecard for its acceptance requirement. Use the worklist for the specific proof and QA #2 for the master defect history.
2. Reuse evidence at its tested SHA. Where a reproducible failure exists, change the smallest source or evidence path that can address it and exercise the relevant positive and negative cases.
3. If the decision depends on Windows, use the smallest existing synthetic fixture that can answer it. Keep a full app journey for a question that requires the full journey. A restricted operation follows its exact owner approval, source and environment.
4. Publish the evidenced result and the next decision in the handoff. Record the job conclusion, last evidenced stage and gate verdict independently. Preserve the tested SHA when later commits only update the record.
