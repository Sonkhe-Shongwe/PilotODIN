**Purpose:** one genuine, permitted business decision, not an invented hypothetical. Fill **NOT SUPPLIED** for unknown facts, **NOT APPLICABLE** with a reason only if genuinely irrelevant. A source citation or non-sensitive descriptive record may be enough for initial triage; independent verification is still required before a PASS.
