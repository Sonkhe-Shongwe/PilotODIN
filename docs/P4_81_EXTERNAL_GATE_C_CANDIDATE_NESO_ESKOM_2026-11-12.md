## Decision: prefer NESO for a research-only, publicly auditable candidate

**Recommended candidate:** GB **National Energy System Operator (NESO)** national **day-ahead electricity-demand forecasting**. The real operational question is how to anticipate grid demand in order to plan electricity supply and balancing. The actual organisational owner of operational decisions is NESO's control/forecasting operation, **not the PilotODIN developer**. NESO's official ENCC description explicitly maps forecasting teams, control-room planning and dispatch:
- [NESO: What does a day in the Electricity National Control Centre look like?](https://www.neso.energy/what-we-do/systems-operations/what-does-day-encc-look)
- [NESO day-ahead forecast and historic data](https://www.neso.energy/data-portal/1-day-ahead-demand-forecast)
- [NESO historic forecast fields with forecast timestamp](https://www.neso.energy/data-portal/1-day-ahead-demand-forecast/historic_day_ahead_demand_forecasts)
- [Elexon/BMRS demand forecasts and comparable INDO outturn](https://bmrs.elexon.co.uk/demand-forecast)
- [Elexon/BMRS NDF publish timestamp API](https://bmrs.elexon.co.uk/api-documentation/endpoint/datasets/NDF)
- [Elexon/BMRS API endpoint catalogue with forecast history/outturn](https://bmrs.elexon.co.uk/api-documentation/endpoint)
- [NESO Open Data Licence v1.0](https://www.neso.energy/data-portal/neso-open-licence)
- [NESO innovation partner channel](https://www.neso.energy/about/innovation/get-involved-neso-innovation) and [innovation strategy with proposal invitation](https://www.neso.energy/about/innovation/innovation-strategy)

**Why this route is viable for *research***: a named system operator, an explicit operational demand-balancing problem, official historical forecasts with forecast timestamps and official externally provided outturns, and open reuse terms **for specific data resources marked as covered by the NESO licence**. The licence expressly permits adaptation and commercial/noncommercial use of *licensed data* with attribution, requires non-endorsement, excludes unlicensed/third-party material and conveys **no operational authority**. Check licence status per resource and Elexon conditions independently; do not assume Elexon data automatically inherits NESO licence.

**Existing project work should be reused, not duplicated:** earlier P4-65/66/67/68/70 already investigated NESO/Elexon as-of records and cross-source verification, including strict release-time/DST controls, and did **not** establish independent authorised operational decision utility. P4-61 shadow-rule CI feasibility is also not independent human action evidence. This P4-81 source search verifies a credible *collaboration route*, not a new fit/forecast or retroactive Gate C PASS.

## NESO candidate translated into the eight Gate C intake requirements

| Check | What public material independently supplies | What is NOT established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **B1 decision owner** | NESO forecast/control centre is a genuine named operator responsible for forecasting and balancing | **No NESO representative has accepted a PilotODIN pilot, approved a proposed decision or signed off an intervention** |
| **B2 operational intervention** | Planning generation and balancing supply with demand is a real process | An authorised, safely bounded choice/actions that PilotODIN would change; cost/constraints and actual integration pathway |
| **B3 target and label** | Potential target: national demand in MW per settlement period; official Elexon **INDO outturn** can be a comparator to forecast NDF; NESO publication archives contain national day-ahead forecasts | Selected final labels, revisions, reporting delays and exact target definition reconciled across sources for each selected record; no source rows fetched/tested in this research turn |
| **B4 as-of predictors** | NESO forecast history includes `FORECAST_TIMESTAMP`; Elexon NDF feed carries `publishTime` | Independent clock-verified **all** proposed feature availability at a frozen decision cutoff, including weather/calendar/external revisions. Archived timestamps can include recovered messages published after original forecast; those must be flagged, not assumed contemporaneous |
| **B5 splitting and leakage** | Time-ordered forecast/outturn records and settlement periods support a proposed chronological evaluation | Independently precommitted horizon/cutoff, per-day grouping (many settlement periods per day), DST and leakage exclusions, untouched holdout and external validity |
| **B6 non-model baseline** | Existing published NESO NDF forecasts are a credible **operational comparator**, but may themselves use models | A *separate* existing rule/process or otherwise honest no-model reference, comparable as-of data horizon and precommitted evaluation; do not misclassify official forecast as a non-model baseline |
| **B7 decision utility** | The real operational decision is documented; published forecasts permit retrospective error study | **No demonstrated avoided system cost, dispatch improvement, forecast-to-action causal value, operator experiment or signoff**. Better MAE would not by itself verify benefit |
| **B8 rights and fairness** | NESO-specific open licence permits reuse under conditions for licensed datasets; non-endorsement is explicit | Licence-by-resource confirmation, separate Elexon terms, operational permission, system safety and independent regulatory/impact review |

**Proposed *research-only* evaluation design, no new fitting authorised:** pick one explicit day-ahead issued-at cutoff; freeze inclusion only for feature snapshots actually timestamped before cutoff; target matching outturn; chronological train/evaluation periods grouped by operating date; compare to official as-of forecast and separately predeclared naive reference; record signed error and operational metrics. Candidate source rows must be acquired in a separately justified, strictly read-only and licence-compliant operation. The one-use PilotODIN research model-fit approval is **CONSUMED**. No additional model fitting, dispatch recommendations, integration or original-owner data operations follows from this plan.

**Concrete path toward genuine signoff:** NESO explicitly invites proposals from organisations, academia and researchers via its [Get involved in NESO Innovation](https://www.neso.energy/about/innovation/get-involved-neso-innovation) page, including its **Big Idea** route and innovation team. The first approach would request a read-only, non-operational validation discussion, confirmation of real decision-time information and what evidence an independent operator would consider useful. Their acknowledgement, participation and approval must be observed, not inferred from the web page. There is **no outreach or submitted proposal in P4-81** and no accepted partner.
